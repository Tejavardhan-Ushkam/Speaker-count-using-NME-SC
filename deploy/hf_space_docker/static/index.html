About the model

  1. Speech detection (pyannote) finds where someone is talking.
  2. Speech is cut into overlapping 1.5 s windows.
  3. Each window becomes a 192-number voice fingerprint (ECAPA-TDNN speaker embedding).
  4. Windows are linked to their most similar neighbours in a graph, and NME-SC spectral clustering groups them; the number of groups is read from the largest jump in the graph's eigenvalues (the eigengap).
  5. The groups are the speakers; their windows give the timeline below.
